## Framing

Strength of schedule shouldn't drive picks on its own, but it's a useful **tiebreaker between closely ranked players**. Focus primarily on (a) **early-season** schedules and (b) **the extremes** — cited example: Drake Maye last year, whose schedule was so easy all season that people are now calling him overrated.

---

## Softest Schedules 📈

### Eagles QB Jalen Hurts
Easiest schedule of any QB in fantasy-matchup terms, per **FantraxHQ's Ryan Prosick**.
- Six games vs. the NFC East — WAS, DAL, NYG were all top-11 in fantasy points allowed to QBs.
- Three more vs. teams top-7 in points allowed to QBs: CHI, PIT, TEN.
- **Downside:** SEA, HOU, SF in the fantasy playoffs → **notable trade-deadline sell candidate** after a hot start.

### Vikings QB Kyler Murray / WRs Justin Jefferson & Jordan Addison
2nd-easiest schedule vs. opposing pass defenses by 2025 EPA allowed per dropback, per **Player Profiler's Jon Machado**. Several vulnerable non-division defenses, and GB / CHI / DET all ranked outside the top pass defenses last season.

### Texans RB David Montgomery
Three of the top-8 easiest opponents for fantasy RBs from last season in his first four games (BUF, CIN, DAL), per **Yahoo's Justin Boone**. This offense is one season removed from Joe Mixon finishing RB5 overall with 1,325 scrimmage yards and 12 TDs.

### Ravens WR Zay Flowers
Per Boone: hot start available again with the Colts, Cowboys and Titans early — all top-7 in fantasy points allowed to WRs in 2025.

### Browns TE Harold Fannin Jr.
First five matchups are all vs. bottom-13 defenses in fantasy points allowed to TEs, two of them bottom-5 (TB, NYJ), per **Fantasy Life's Owen Oates**. Four of the five (JAX, TB, CAR, NYJ) ranked significantly better against WRs, so whoever is at QB should look Fannin's way early and often. Schedule gets much tougher after that.

### All Detroit Lions
Easiest overall schedule in the league, per **Warren Sharp's SoS analysis**. Indoors or warm weather for every game but one (Week 17 @ CHI). Defense lost three secondary starters → poised for plenty of shootouts.

### D/STs to Target
- **Baltimore Ravens** — most favorable D/ST schedule of the season. Only DAL (Wk 3), CIN (Wks 7 & 17) and BUF (Wk 8) stand out as tough; **12 of 17 matchups grade average or better**, per ESPN's Tristan H. Cockcroft.
- **L.A. Chargers** — host Arizona and Las Vegas to open.
- **Green Bay Packers** — Week 1 @ MIN is meh, then Jets and Falcons.
- **Cincinnati Bengals** — usually brutal on defense, but all five opening opponents grade middling-to-good as D/ST matchups: TB, HOU, PIT, JAX, MIA (Cockcroft).

## Toughest Schedules 📉

### Panthers QB Bryce Young / WRs Tetairoa McMillan & Jalen Coker
Tied for 3rd-toughest overall schedule; non-division slate loaded with BAL, DEN, SEA, PHI, DET, GB, CHI, MIN. Isolating pass defenses, Carolina faces the **single toughest** schedule by 2025 EPA allowed per dropback (Machado).

### Jaguars WR Brian Thomas Jr.
Likely the team's X receiver → lines up out wide against the opponent's top corner most weeks.

### 49ers TE George Kittle
Coming off an Achilles tear in last season's Wild Card round, so the consensus expects a slow start and a late surge — but his **fantasy playoff opponents ranked 32nd, 28th and 26th in points allowed to TEs** last season, per NBC Sports' Kyle Dvorchak. Also: the 49ers set an NFL record this year traveling **38,105 miles through 58 time zones**, across three countries including two international games (per KTXL's Asia Cymone Smith).

### Cardinals RB Jeremiyah Love
Arizona has the **hardest overall SoS in the NFL** — not favored in a single game this season — plus one of the hardest schedules against run defenses (Machado). Constant negative game scripts means passing volume, not carries, for the rookie RB.

### Giants RB Cam Skattebo
First six weeks are favorable (**4th-easiest start**), but no team plays a tougher slate of **road** games (LAR, WAS, HOU, PHI, IND, SEA, DET, DAL), per FantasyPros' Andrew Erickson. Hard to find positive game scripts down the stretch.

### All Cleveland Browns
**Seven outdoor games from Week 10 onward:** home vs. HOU (Wk 10), LV (Wk 12), CIN (Wk 13), ATL (Wk 14), IND (Wk 17); road at NYG (Wk 15) and BAL (Wk 16), per FTN Fantasy's Colin McTamany.

### D/STs to Fade
- **Houston Texans** — possibly the best overall defense in the league, still draftable, but the opener is brutal (BUF Wk 1, CIN Wk 2, DAL Wk 4) — a real problem in Guillotine/Chopped leagues. Tough fantasy playoffs too: JAX, PHI, GB.
- **Denver Broncos** — brutal opening slate (KC, JAX, LAR, SF, LAC, SEA) and tough semifinal/championship matchups vs. BUF and NE.

## Rookie Spotlight 🔦 — Malachi Fields (WR, Giants)

- **Current ADP:** WR73 · **Morning Huddle rank:** WR67
- 6'4", 218 lbs. A "tape" prospect, not a "data" prospect — 4.61 40, only 4.6 YAC average (−0.3 over expected after adjusting for aDOT), and one of the worst targeted-QB passer ratings in the class, per Fantasy Life's Dwain McFarland.
- Has reportedly won a starting spot opposite Malik Nabers, which is more than most rookies can say.
- Verdict: intriguing red-zone target, but most likely the **4th target** on the team — a better football player than fantasy option.
