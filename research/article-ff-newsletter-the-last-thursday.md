## Draft Assistant, Part 2 (their tool)

Three features they hadn't covered:
1. **Survival odds** — calculates the probability a given player is still on the board at your next pick. A TE who's 80% likely to survive two rounds vs. an RB who's a coin flip changes the pick even if the RB is nominally BPA.
2. **Draft Score (0–100)** on every available player, so you can see at a glance whether a pick is clear-cut or a close call, with the reasoning one click away.
3. **Instant draft grade** on the Summary tab after the final pick — best values, playoff stacks, positional strengths, plus a season simulator for playoff/championship odds.
